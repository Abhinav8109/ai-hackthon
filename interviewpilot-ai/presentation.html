SLIDE 02 / 06
Industry Challenge

The Screening Interview Dilemma

Senior engineering teams are losing up to 20 hours a week on unqualified candidate screening.

❌ Traditional Screening Bottlenecks

• Generic Questionnaires: Irrelevant to what the candidate actually built.
• Unchallenged Buzzwords: Shallow answers pass through without probing.
• Subjective Grading: Inconsistent scoring across different interviewers.
• Costly Distraction: Engineers pulled away from shipping product.

✅ The InterviewPilot AI Solution

• Resume-Anchored Probing: Evaluates exact claims (e.g. latency, scale).
• Adaptive Intercept: Detects answers <60% complete and triggers follow-up.
• Objective Evidence: Scores backed by verbatim quotes.
• 70% Time Savings: Engineers only interview validated finalists.

SLIDE 03 / 06
Observable Execution

Under the Hood: 5 Real Python Tools

No hardcoded logs. The agent executes real deterministic tools on every answer turn.

Tool Name Trigger Condition Action & Output
search_resume_claims() Question formulation & Turn start Extracts verified achievements matching target tech stack.
evaluate_answer() Every candidate submission Scores 1–5 against rubrics; measures completeness percentage.
validate_score_evidence() Post-evaluation Verifies score is grounded in verbatim quote words (no hallucination).
check_competency_coverage() Turn completion Monitors depth across all 5 core competency domains.
detect_consistency() Interview wrap-up Audits resume claims: Supported vs Needs Clarification.
